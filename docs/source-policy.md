# Source policy

CatalogIQ accepts only public `http` and `https` URLs. It rejects URL credentials, localhost, loopback, private, reserved, multicast, unspecified and link-local ranges. Every redirect is re-evaluated. DNS failure is conservatively untrusted.

Amazon, eBay, Walmart, AliExpress and Etsy are prohibited. Manufacturer webpages and PDFs receive trust 1.0; approved public third parties receive lower prototype trust. Retrieval records URL, domain, timestamp, content type, status, SHA-256 hash and policy outcome. Manufacturer ownership is explicitly configured, never guessed.
